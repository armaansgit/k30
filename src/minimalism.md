# Minimalism

In 2026, minimalism often refers to high end products. Everything is expected to be sleek, lightweight, rich in texture, and expensive.

But that is not what minimalism really is.

True minimalism is about surviving with what you already have, whether it is an old phone, a very old laptop, or an old camera. It is about living with only what you need and not constantly upgrading or going beyond your actual needs.

That is what **minimalism** truly means.
