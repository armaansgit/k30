# Isolation

It is not for everyone, but when it’s really enough for a human, they seek peace. However, they often struggle to find it because they seek it while surrounded by distractions.

One of the best ways to seek peace is to step away from all the distractions (we all know it’s hard, as it should be), but it’s really worth it.

It was always about what’s *important* versus what’s **more important**.
