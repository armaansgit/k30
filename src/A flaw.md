# A Flaw

*This post is all about "A Flaw" in me which I am trying to fix*

january (2026) - completed 10,000 pushups

a image here from my website


## Context

I gave my self a challenge in (2025) - November.

I did 10,000 pushups. Started at 3, climbed to 250 a day, 
missed one day in the entire run. Hit the target on January 31st 
and never touched a pushup again. Not once.

### Flaw

That's the flaw. I don't chase goals. I chase proof.
The moment I proved I could do something, my brain files it away and moves on. 
The goal was never fitness.

Because from start I was told that I am weak and I can't do pushups 
maybe because of that I was just trying to prove myself that anything ispossible.
